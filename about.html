About Anees Institution

Education and skill training for students and learners in Kinathukadavu.

A learning centre that works with you, not past you

Anees Institution offers tuition for school students and practical training in two traditional skills, Aari work and Mehendi, all in one centre.

We keep learning practical and personal. Students get attention that matches their pace, and skill learners spend their class time practising, not just watching.

Our aim is simple: help students do well in their exams, and help learners finish a course with a skill they can use for their career.

Practical learning, personal attention and career growth are what we focus on.

What we focus on

Understanding first

We teach concepts step by step and test often, so progress is visible to students and parents.

Every student counts

Weak students get special focus, and slow learners have a separate batch.

Skills that earn

Aari work and Mehendi courses end with real practice on designs people actually ask for.
