Questions parents and students ask

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Which classes do you teach?

We teach school students from 6th to 12th, with tutorial classes for 10th and 12th. For classes 1 to 5, call or WhatsApp us to ask about batches.

Do you offer a demo class?

Yes. A free demo class is available, so you can see how we teach before registering.

What is the fee for Aari work training?

The Aari work course is 2 months long. The fee is ₹4000 and materials are free.

What is the fee for Mehendi training?

The Mehendi course is 1 month long. The fee is ₹1000.

Will I learn bridal designs?

Yes. The Mehendi course goes from basic patterns to bridal designs, including Arabic and traditional styles.

What if my child is a slow learner?

We run a special batch for slow learners and also offer individual coaching, so each student can learn at their own pace.

Do you help with board exams?

Yes. We offer board exam preparation for 10th and 12th, including a crash course, daily practice and regular tests.

How do I register?

Fill the admission form on the Register page. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and we will reply to confirm your batch. You can also call us on +91 88078 56958.

Where is the centre?

We are at Periyar Nagar 1st Street, Kinathukadavu, Coimbatore - 642109. Find the map on the Contact page.
